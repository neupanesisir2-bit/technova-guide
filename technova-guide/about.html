OUR PURPOSE

Technology should feel understandable.

TechNova Guide is an independent educational publication focused on practical technology, artificial intelligence, Linux, computer networking and cybersecurity.

What we aim to publish

We create beginner-friendly explainers, step-by-step tutorials and carefully researched guides. Our goal is to help readers understand how things work, make informed decisions and build useful digital skills.

Our editorial approach

  • Explain concepts in clear, accessible language.
  • Prefer practical examples over unexplained jargon.
  • Check technical details and update articles when important information changes.
  • Be transparent about limitations, sponsorships and affiliate relationships.
TechNova Guide is a learning resource. Always verify important security, financial or technical decisions against authoritative documentation.

Editorial contact: For corrections and suggestions, open an issue in our public GitHub repository. Please do not include passwords, private keys or other sensitive information.
